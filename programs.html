Understand Qualification Levels & Frameworks

Educational qualifications are structured through national and international frameworks that define learning outcomes, academic standards, and progression pathways.

Understanding Qualification Levels

Explore the frameworks that standardize and validate educational qualifications across Sri Lanka and internationally.

Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework (SLQF)

A nationally recognized framework that standardizes higher education qualifications from certificate level to doctoral studies. The SLQF defines the expected learning outcomes, credit requirements, and academic standards for each qualification level, ensuring consistency and quality across Sri Lankan higher education institutions.

National Vocational Qualification (NVQ)

A competency-based qualification system designed to support vocational and technical education pathways. NVQ qualifications are structured across multiple levels, from basic operational skills to advanced professional competencies, providing flexible progression routes for students pursuing technical and practical career paths.

International Frameworks

Explore qualification structures from the United Kingdom and other globally recognized education systems. International frameworks such as the UK's Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) and the European Qualifications Framework (EQF) provide standardized benchmarks for comparing qualifications across borders, facilitating international mobility and recognition.
